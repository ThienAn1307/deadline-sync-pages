Deadline Sync

Deadline Sync is a personal tool that collects course deadlines from Moodle, Google Classroom, Slack and Outlook, and adds them to a dedicated calendar in its owner's Google Calendar.

It runs on its developer's own computer and is used only with the developer's own accounts. It is not offered as a service to other people.

How it uses Google data

  • Google Classroom (read-only): reads the signed-in user's courses, coursework due dates, their own submission status and course announcements.
  • Google Calendar: creates one secondary calendar and adds, updates or removes deadline events in that calendar only.
  • Gmail (read-only): reads only Slack's notification emails, to catch deadlines that lecturers post on Slack.

Details are in the privacy policy.
