Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Deadline Sync ("the app") is a personal tool that its developer runs on their own computer, for their own accounts. This policy explains how the app handles data it receives through Google APIs.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Classroom (read-only): the list of courses, coursework titles, descriptions and due dates, the signed-in user's own submission status, and course announcements.
  • Google Calendar: a secondary calendar created by the app, and the events in that calendar.
  • Gmail (read-only): only emails sent by Slack (from slack.com) in the last two weeks, to find Slack notifications from the lecturers the user follows. No other email is read, and nothing in Gmail is changed.

How the data is used

The data is used only to create deadline events in the user's own Google Calendar and keep them up to date. The app does not read or change any other calendar.

Storage

OAuth tokens and a small local database of synced deadlines are stored only on the user's own computer. The app has no server and keeps no copy of Google data anywhere else.

Sharing

Google user data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone. To find the deadlines in them, the text of the Slack notification emails described above, and any text the user pastes in, is sent to the Google Gemini API, which only returns the deadlines it finds; the app stores those deadlines, not the emails. No other data from Gmail, and nothing from Google Calendar or Google Classroom, is sent to an AI model.

Limited Use

Deadline Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local folder removes all stored tokens and data. The calendar the app created can be deleted from Google Calendar settings.
